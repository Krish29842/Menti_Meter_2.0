- `time_remaining` computed **server-side** = `deadline - submitted_at`
- Clamped to `[0, time_limit]`
- `BASE` configurable per quiz (default 1000)

**Leaderboard storage:**
- Redis Sorted Set: `leaderboard:{session_id}` → member = participant_id, score = cumulative points
- Postgres `participant.total_score` is authoritative on session end (reconciled from responses)

**Tie-breaking** (applied at read time):
1. Higher total score
2. More correct answers
3. Lower cumulative submission time
