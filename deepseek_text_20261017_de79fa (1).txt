
- **Redis Pub/Sub** decouples pods: any pod handling a WS client subscribes to the session channel.
- **Session lock** (Redis `SETNX` with TTL) ensures only one pod drives state transitions (start, next-question, close).
- **Timers**: question close timer stored in Redis (`SET question:{id}:deadline`); a worker or scheduled job closes it, broadcasting `question:closed`.
